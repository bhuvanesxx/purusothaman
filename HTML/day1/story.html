I’ll keep it natural, like you’re telling the story to a friend

Last time, I planned a Munnar trip with my college friends. At first, some people weren’t sure if they were coming or not, but in the end, everyone decided to come. There was also one girl who said she would come only in the morning, so we all started early in the morning in a van. On the way, we broke a coconut and started the trip with some dancing and fun. After around 45 km, we stopped for tea. At that time, one guy played the speaker at full volume and suddenly the van had a problem. We couldn’t continue without the speaker, so we had to change the van. By then, everyone was already getting frustrated, but somehow we continued and finally reached Munnar. After reaching our room, the van driver asked us to buy some alcohol for him, so we bought it. We also bought around five bottles for ourselves and continued the trip. After drinking, everyone started dancing and having fun. Later, the girls also joined us. Then one person started vomiting, and the whole night became a bit chaotic. There were also a few couples in the group who were roaming around together. The next morning, we went sightseeing around Munnar. We took photos on Gap Road and enjoyed the views. After that, we headed towards Kollukumalai. At Kollukumalai, one of the girls asked for alcohol because she wanted to drink. She said she would drink with one of the guys, so we bought it. After drinking, everyone was dancing and having fun. Later, most of the group went to sleep, but four of us stayed awake and continued drinking. Our accommodation there was a tent. That girl and one guy went somewhere to drink together, while me and another friend were drinking nearby. After some time, those two came back after drinking, and suddenly something went wrong. The girl started shouting and behaving strangely. Then she suddenly started acting like she was possessed, and the guy also started behaving the same way. We were all drunk and didn’t really know what to do. I tried to hold and control the guy while my two friends tried to control the girl. This went on from around 1:30 AM to 4:15 AM. We had to stay awake and keep an eye on them because we were worried they might hurt themselves or someone else. At one point, another guy tried to control the girl, but he also started behaving strangely. We were completely confused and didn’t know what was happening. Around 4 AM, when things finally started getting under control, everyone was exhausted. At around 4:15 AM, it was time for sightseeing, and the rest of the group went. But me, my friend, that girl, and that guy had basically spent the whole night awake trying to handle everything. After that, we eventually went back, checked out the next day, and returned home. It was honestly one of the craziest and most unforgettable trips I’ve ever had.
